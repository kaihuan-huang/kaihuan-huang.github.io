IPOT Restaurant Platform

A reservation service real guests use, the POS money path, and Nalu, the bilingual booking agent.真实客人在用的预订服务、POS 资金链路，以及双语预订助手 Nalu。

Role角色

Stack技术栈
Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Alembic · Twilio Verify · Ollama · AWS
Status状态
Reservation API in production · Nalu in pre-launch testingReservation API 已上线 · Nalu 预发布测试中

01Problem问题

A growing restaurant group needed bookings, payments and guest messaging that do not break on retries, double taps or a model that guesses.一个成长中的餐饮集团，需要在重试、重复点击和模型乱猜时都不出错的预订、支付与客人消息。

02What I built我做了什么

  • Reservation API: capacity-based booking, Twilio Verify phone OTP, SMS with delivery-status tracking, token-based self-service reschedule and cancel.Reservation API：按容量预订、Twilio Verify 手机验证码、带送达状态跟踪的短信、基于令牌的自助改期与取消。
  • POS: atomic settlement and refunds, sale-time price snapshots, audited voids; revived a long-failing test suite and fixed diverged Alembic heads.POS：原子结算与退款、售出时价格快照、可审计作废；修复长期失败的测试套件与分叉的 Alembic heads。
  • Nalu: deterministic field resolution and an explicit-yes gate after the LLM pilot invented values.Nalu：LLM 试点编造字段后，改为确定性字段解析加“明确说是”闸门。
  • Google Ads and Meta daily spend importers for the revenue agent.为收入 Agent 编写 Google Ads 与 Meta 每日花费导入器。
