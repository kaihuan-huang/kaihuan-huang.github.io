Light-Up

Giving that shows its impact: match donors to causes, tell each donor whose life their gift changed, and connect the charities behind it.让捐款看得见影响：为捐款人匹配项目，告诉每位捐款人他的捐助改变了谁，并把背后的慈善机构连接起来。

Role角色
Founder and engineer发起人兼工程师
Stack技术栈
2023: React prototype2023 年：React 原型
Status状态
Concept from November 2023; restarted in 2026 and in development2023 年 11 月提出构想；2026 年重启，开发中

01Problem问题

Donors rarely see what their gift did, choosing a cause is guesswork, and small charities work alone. In 2023 the three things that would fix this — a personal impact story for every donor, matching donors to causes, and a way to connect charities — needed AI that could not yet do them well. It can now, so I kept the project.捐款人很少知道自己的捐款带来了什么，选择项目全凭感觉，小型慈善机构也各自为战。2023 年能解决这些问题的三件事——给每位捐款人一份个性化的影响故事、为捐款人匹配项目、把慈善机构连接起来——都需要当时的 AI 还做不好的能力。现在可以了，所以我留下了这个项目。

02What I built我做了什么

  • 2023: defined the concept and started a React prototype.2023 年：确定产品构想，开始做 React 原型。
  • Personal impact stories for each donor, written from what the charity actually reports.为每位捐款人生成个性化的影响故事，内容依据慈善机构的实际报告。
  • Matching donors to causes from their stated interests and values.根据捐款人填写的兴趣和价值观匹配项目。
  • A shared directory so charities can find and work with each other.一个共享目录，让慈善机构能互相发现、彼此协作。
