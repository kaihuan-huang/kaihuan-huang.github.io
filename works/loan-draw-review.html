Loan-Draw Review Controls

Money leaves only after two people sign off, the same draw cannot be funded twice, and every step lands in a verifiable audit chain.资金只在两人签字后放出，同一笔提款不能重复放款，每一步都写入可验证的审计链。

Role
AI Engineer on a team. I wrote the review controls and workflow reliability layer; the AI flagging pipeline is teammates' work团队中的 AI 工程师。我编写审核控制与工作流可靠性层；AI 标记管道由同事完成
Stack
Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · SQLAlchemy · Alembic · Temporal
Status
Built and tested; pull requests open / in draft已构建并测试；PR 开放 / 草稿中

01Problem问题

Construction-loan draws are paid in stages. An AI check can flag a missing invoice, but the decision to wire money has to stay with people, and a retry or a race must never fund a draw twice.建筑贷款按阶段放款。AI 可以标记缺失的发票，但汇款决定必须留在人手里，重试或竞态也绝不能让同一笔提款放两次。

02What I built我做了什么

  • Two-person (maker-checker) approval for higher-risk draws, behind a feature flag.高风险提款采用双人（制单-复核）审批，置于功能开关之后。
  • Loan-level PostgreSQL row locks, a repeat-funding check and a partial unique index on the wire reference.贷款级 PostgreSQL 行锁、重复放款检查，以及电汇编号上的部分唯一索引。
  • Hash-chained audit records with a verification routine.带验证程序的哈希链审计记录。
  • Transactional outbox and Temporal recovery for draw verification, with a partial unique index that prevents two verification runs at once.为提款核验加入事务性 outbox 与 Temporal 恢复，并用部分唯一索引防止两次核验同时运行。
