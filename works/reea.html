REEA

A real-estate platform for buyers, sellers and agents: listings and search, plus a GPT-3 tool that writes listing content.面向买家、卖家和经纪人的房地产平台：房源与搜索，外加 AI 撰写的房源内容。

Role角色
Founding engineer. Built the platform, worked with contract developers, and contributed to the product plan and pitch deck创始工程师。搭建平台，与外包开发者协作，参与产品规划和融资演示稿
Stack技术栈
Python · Django 4.1 · django-allauth · OpenAI GPT-3 API · SQLite / PostgreSQL
Status状态
Early-stage product; code private; not running today早期产品；代码私有；目前未运行

01Problem问题

Buyers struggle to pin down what they want and get little personal attention from agents, while listings and paperwork stay scattered. The bet: AI tools could make the process faster and more transparent for buyers, sellers and agents.买家很难说清自己想要什么，也很少得到经纪人的个别关注；房源和文件又分散各处。我们的判断是：AI 工具能让买家、卖家和经纪人都更快、更透明。

02What I built我做了什么

  • A Django platform with accounts (sign-up, login, password reset), structured property listings (type, status, rooms, amenities, price, video) and search.Django 平台：账户（注册、登录、找回密码）、结构化房源（类型、状态、房间、设施、价格、视频）和搜索。
  • An AI content studio: three GPT-3 steps — topic ideas, section titles, section write-ups — with a monthly word allowance per subscriber.AI 内容工作台：GPT-3 分三步生成——选题、章节标题、章节正文——每个订阅用户有每月字数额度。
  • Product work alongside the code: competitor research, a B2B white-label versus B2C model, and the pitch deck.代码之外的产品工作：竞品研究、B2B 白标与 B2C 两种模式，以及融资演示稿。
  • Built with contract developers; I wrote 68 of the 95 commits.与外包开发者共同完成；95 个提交中 68 个由我编写。
